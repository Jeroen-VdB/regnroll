Certificate upload

Upload the public part of your new certificate (.cer or .pem). It will be added to your application's registration — your current certificate is not removed, so you can switch over at your own pace.

Never upload a private key or a PFX/PKCS#12 file. Only the public certificate is needed, and uploads containing private key material are rejected.
